## Question

Which Questionnaires launch, and on what scoring? Give each candidate one status:

- **In scope at launch:** questions and scoring rules are in hand, and their source is named.
- **Deferred until the rules arrive:** it follows after launch.
- **Not a Questionnaire:** its page is content, handled in [Keep, merge or drop each current page](19-keep-merge-drop.md).

The candidates are the KarmaYoga Scale, the Dosha Survey, the 16 pages that describe a Questionnaire, `/teertha-yatra-quiz/`, `/surveys/`, and any Questionnaire found only in archives.

**Who decides the scoring**

- Where sources disagree, the Psychology expert decides which counts.
- With no Psychology expert named, only the documented current scoring may be used, matched exactly (the brief's Roles fallback).
- A published key, or a scale found only in the literature, means deferral until an expert decides.

**For the fallback, settle:**

- which written record counts as the documented current scoring, such as the KarmaYoga answer key decoded from the page
- the standard limitations wording: carried over unchanged, not reviewed for validity, no claim of validity, not a diagnosis

**At launch**

Decide what the Questionnaires section and each deferred Questionnaire's page say, without promising dates.
