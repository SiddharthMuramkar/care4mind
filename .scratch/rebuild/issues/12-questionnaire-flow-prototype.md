## Question

How should taking a Questionnaire look and behave? Coding agents build a throwaway interactive mock using the 45 KarmaYoga Scale items (or placeholders). It should be polished, but not tied to a visual style, which [Design direction](21-design-direction.md) sets later. The developer tries it at 360 and 1440 px, by keyboard and with a screen reader, and reacts.

Cover:

- the purpose and limitations screen shown before it starts
- one page or several, and how progress shows
- refusing incomplete answers without losing what was entered, and going back
- the results page: the result, interpretation, limitations, "not a diagnosis", retake and print
- motion and feedback that make it engaging without trivialising a mental-health subject

Assume nothing is stored and the result's URL carries no answers. Placeholder text stands in for the wording of purpose, limitations and interpretation, which belongs to the Psychology expert.

The developer decides layout and interaction. Link the prototype from the answer.
