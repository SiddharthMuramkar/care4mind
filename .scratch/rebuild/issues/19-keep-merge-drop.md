## Question

What survives from the current site?

- Decide keep, merge (into what) or drop for each of the 33 pages and 6 posts.
- Apply a stated rule for placeholders, duplicates and empty pages:
  - `/sample-page/`, `/home/` and `/home-2/`
  - the two Text Books pages
  - `/surveys/`, `/teertha-yatra-quiz/` and `/resources/`
  - `/anubandha-chatushtaya/` and `/webpages-and-av-material/`
- Map each kept item to its content type.
- Treat these as content, whatever happens to their Questionnaires: the 16 pages that describe a Questionnaire, `/karmayoga-scale/` and `/dosha-survey/`.
- Cover site-wide text too: the header, footer and tagline, the empty social links, and the homepage cards that point at the dead app.

Dropped pages still get a redirect (see [URLs and redirects](24-urls-and-redirects.md)).

The developer decides placeholders and duplicates. Every call on real content is flagged for the Project owner in the sign-off pack.
