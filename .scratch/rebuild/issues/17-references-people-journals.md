## Question

How are References, and the people behind them, recorded?

**References**

- Which kinds exist (journal article, book, chapter, thesis, website, audio-visual item), and which fields each needs under FR-03?
- Are References shared records with their own pages?
- Are abstracts stored, or only linked?
- What does "evidence type" mean, and what are its allowed values?
- How do the Text Books, Inspirational Books and "Webpages and AV material" lists map onto References?

**People and journals**

- Are authors, translators, traditional authors (such as Patañjali) and Concept contributors one person record, or plain names?
- Do journals get records?
- Do people and journals get pages listing their works, for FR-04's search by author?

**Citation style**

Pick one that has a maintained CSL style (CSL is the standard machine-readable citation format) and handles classical works cited by verse. APA 7 and Chicago author-date are examples. The Research editor confirms it in the sign-off pack.
