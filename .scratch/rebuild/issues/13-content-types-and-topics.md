## Question

Which content types does the site have, and how are they grouped into topics? Decide:

- whether a Model or technique is its own type or a kind of Concept
- whether an "application" (FR-01) is a type with its own pages, a field, or a section of text
- which other types are needed: plain page, post, course or institute, curated lists such as Text Books
- what a topic is:
  - flat or nested
  - whether each Concept has one primary topic that sets its breadcrumb
  - whether traditional texts, References and models belong to topics too
- whether a Concept can group others, as pañca kleśa groups its five kleśas
- whether keywords form one shared list
- which fields every type shares:
  - review status: its states, whether it controls publishing or only labels content, and what carried-over content gets (for example "carried over, not reviewed")
  - dates
  - contributor

The model here is platform-neutral: types, fields and links, not database tables. Assume a meaning for "applications" until the client's reply to [Send the client the question list](01-client-question-list.md) arrives; the sign-off pack confirms it.

The developer decides, and the Research editor reviews the topic list in the sign-off pack. Update GLOSSARY.md as terms settle (topic, Model or technique, application, review status).
