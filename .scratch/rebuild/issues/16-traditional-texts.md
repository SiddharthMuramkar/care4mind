## Question

How are traditional texts recorded? Decide:

- whether a text is one work with child passages
- how a passage is:
  - located (for example YS 1.30)
  - stored (Devanagari, IAST or both)
  - translated (one translation or several, each with its translator and edition)
- whether editions and translations are themselves References
- whether commentaries are in or out (the client brief lists them; the question list asks)
- whether each passage gets its own page
- whether a Concept links to a whole work or to particular passages
- whether the shlokas on current pages become passages, or stay inside a Concept's text
