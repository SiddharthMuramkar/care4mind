# Later phases

These ideas are kept for after launch, outside this project's scope. Each came from the UI/UX spec, #166.

- Images in one consistent look (hero, topics, traditional texts). Licences: The Met Open Access is CC0; Unsplash is free but can't be compiled into a competing service, and Unsplash+ is a separate licence; Pexels is free, but no identifiable people in a bad light, no implied endorsement, and no use in a logo or trademark; Wikimedia Commons varies by file; Open Peeps is CC0; unDraw must be self-hosted, and its style is very common. From #166.
- Book covers on the reading lists from the Open Library Covers API: look each cover ID up once at build time, sending a User-Agent, because ISBN lookups are limited to 100 per IP every 5 minutes. From #166.
- Inline meanings: a Sanskrit word in running text shows its gloss on hover or tap. From #166.
- "Breathe with the lotus": a one-minute guided breathing exercise the visitor starts, which ends by itself or with × or Escape, and has no pause toggle. From #166.
- Devanagari first as a site-wide setting, dropped from the build in #43 (#29 closed). From #166.
- An About page. From #166.
