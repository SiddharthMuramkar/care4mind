# Care4Mind

The new care4mind.org, a portal for Indian psychology. This file names the words the project uses, so people and agents mean the same thing by them.

## Pictures

**Emblem**:
The Care4Mind logo picture: a pink lotus whose petals form a brain.
_Avoid_: logo image

**Sticker**:
A small folk-art icon with a cream die-cut edge, showing one central symbol.
_Avoid_: icon, badge

**Pothi**:
A Sticker of a palm-leaf manuscript, one for each Traditional text.
_Avoid_: cover, book cover

**Plate**:
A square Indian folk painting, whether the client's own or made for this site.
_Avoid_: photo, illustration, hero image, scene

**Medallion**:
The way a Plate is shown: cropped to a circle inside a drawn ring. It is not a kind of picture.
_Avoid_: round image, circle

**Frontispiece**:
The Medallion at the head of a topic page.
_Avoid_: banner, hero

**Ornament**:
A border or frame drawn in code rather than kept as a picture file.
_Avoid_: divider image

**Interim picture**:
A stand-in shown at the head of a page until that page's own picture exists.
_Avoid_: placeholder, fallback
